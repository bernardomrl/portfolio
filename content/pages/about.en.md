I write front-end code professionally in React and TypeScript, mostly on
products where the interface carried the whole experience.

What I keep coming back to is the unglamorous part of a codebase: layer
boundaries a linter can enforce, content validated at build time instead of
trusted at runtime, and a `strict` TypeScript configuration nobody is allowed
to escape with `any`.

This site is one of those experiments. It is statically generated, bilingual,
and every architectural decision behind it is written down and versioned — the
source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/bernardomrl/portfolio).
